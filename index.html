// Mission Log

Missions Accomplished

Publish data the business can trust

Built validation-first publishing workflows: records pass data-quality gates (schema, domains, ranges, duplicates) before they are published with a manifest and checksum, so reporting consumers can verify before they load.

Reconcile every break

Designed SQL and Python reconciliation between source systems and published data — matched, amount breaks, and orphans classified and counted, so breaks surface in the morning report instead of in a stakeholder's meeting.

Automate the manual

At Amazon Pricing Operations, automated recurring data and reporting work, freeing the team from repetitive manual effort and cutting error rates.

Guard the gates

Fraud prevention and risk-based authentication analysis for retail operations — protecting legitimate customers while stopping abuse.
